## TIER FIT GUIDE

Based on answers, recommend:

### → ESSENTIALS ($1,250/month)
- Revenue under $500K
- Single location
- No payroll needs (or simple)
- Books are reasonably current
- Just needs compliance + clean books
- Budget-conscious

### → GROWTH ($2,500/month) ⭐
- Revenue $500K - $2M
- Has payroll or wants it
- Needs inventory tracking
- Wants visibility/KPIs
- Growing or planning to grow
- Values advisory input

### → CFO ($5,000/month)
- Revenue $2M+
- Multiple locations
- Complex compensation
- Major decisions ahead (expansion, M&A, financing)
- Wants strategic partner
- High-touch relationship

---

## RED FLAGS (Consider Not Engaging)

⚠️ Books 2+ years behind with no cleanup budget  
⚠️ Unrealistic budget expectations  
⚠️ Prior fraud or serious tax issues  
⚠️ Won't provide software access  
⚠️ Wants hourly billing only  
⚠️ Multiple fired accountants  
⚠️ Disrespectful communication
