Your Books Don't Tell You Anything Useful

Botox, Dysport, filler, laser, facials, retail—all lumped into one "revenue" line. You can't tell which services are actually profitable. You can't see that your Morpheus8 treatments have a 72% margin while your chemical peels are underwater. You're flying blind and making decisions based on gut feel instead of data.

Business and Personal Are Dangerously Tangled

Your personal card bought that new laser. Your business account paid for your car payment. It happens. But it's costing you: the IRS loves to disallow expenses they can't clearly trace, and you're setting yourself up for an audit nightmare. The average audit assessment for "inadequate records" is $12,000.

→ We untangle it and set up systems so it never happens again.

Sales Tax Is a Ticking Time Bomb

Botox injection? Not taxable (it's medical). The syringe you used? Taxable (it's a consumable). The skincare you sold them? Taxable. The skincare you included "free" with a package? Also taxable. Most med spas get this wrong—and the state doesn't send a warning. They send a bill for back taxes plus penalties.

→ We set it up right so you sleep at night.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I need a CPA who specializes in med spas?

Med spas have unique tax situations: injectable inventory tracking, equipment depreciation, sales tax on products vs. services, 1099 vs. W-2 injector classification, medical director agreements. A generalist will miss deductions and make mistakes that cost you $15K–$40K annually.

How much can I actually save?

Most med spas we work with save $15,000–$40,000 per year through proper entity structure, deduction optimization, and proactive tax planning. We'll give you a realistic estimate on your first call—no hype.

I already have an accountant. What's wrong with them?

Maybe nothing. But if they don't specialize in med spas, they're almost certainly missing industry-specific strategies. We see it every week. The first call is free—we'll tell you honestly if switching makes sense for your situation.

Do you work with new med spas?

Yes. Setting up the right entity structure and accounting systems from day one saves massive headaches (and money) later. If you're just starting, that's actually the best time to get this right.

What if I'm in a state you don't serve?

We serve med spas in all 50 states. Everything is virtual—we work with practices from New York to California to everywhere in between. No need to be local.
