Sound Familiar?

Running a med spa comes with unique financial challenges that generic accountants don't understand.

💉 Inventory Headaches

Botox, fillers, and injectables require careful tracking. One wrong count and your margins disappear.

📋 Compliance Confusion

Medical licensing, HIPAA, state regulations — the rules change constantly and the penalties are real.

💸 Revenue Recognition

Memberships, packages, and prepaid treatments make revenue tracking a nightmare.

👥 Provider Compensation

Commission structures, splits, and 1099 vs W-2 decisions can make or break your profitability.

📊 No Industry Benchmarks

Is your 62% labor cost normal? Your accountant has no idea because they don't know med spas.

🏦 Growth Capital

Banks don't understand your business model. Getting financing for expansion feels impossible.
