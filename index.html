Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I need a CPA who specializes in med spas?

Medical spas have unique complexities—cash-pay models, injectable inventory, provider compensation, equipment depreciation. A generalist won't catch the deductions or compliance issues we see every day.

How can you help reduce my taxes?

We use S-Corp structuring, Section 179 depreciation, retirement plan optimization, and year-round planning. Most clients see significant savings in their first year.

Do you work with med spas outside of New York?

Yes. We serve med spa owners in all 50 states. Our cloud-based approach means seamless service regardless of your location.

What's included in fractional CFO services?
