Common Challenges

Sound Familiar?

Med spas face unique financial complexities that most accountants simply don't understand.

💉

Inventory Complexity

Botox, fillers, and injectables require precise tracking. One wrong count and your margins disappear.

Regulatory Maze

Medical licensing, HIPAA, state regulations — the rules change constantly and penalties are steep.

Revenue Recognition

No Benchmarks

Growth Financing
